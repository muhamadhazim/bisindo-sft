Reviewed 2026-09-11. Status: SOURCE_VERIFIED, not VALIDATOR_VERIFIED.

Nine images are copied unchanged from the publisher's `02. Resized Images`

No cropping, mirroring, recoloring, AI image editing, or augmentation was applied.

## Evidence and scope of comparison

- Inspected three publisher-labeled examples per letter, across different
  photographed orientations/backgrounds. All nine images show one hand.
- Cross-checked hand count against section 3.1 of Indra et al., *Indonesian
  Sign Language Recognition Based on Shape of Hand Gesture* (2019), which
  explicitly places C/L/O in its one-hand alphabet group.
  https://doi.org/10.1016/j.procs.2019.11.101
- Cross-checked static classification against section 3.1 (printed page 180,
  PDF page 5) of Damatraseta, Alfan, and Yuliandi, which distinguishes R/J as
  dynamic and the other 24 letters as static in its studied reference.
  https://ojs.uajy.ac.id/index.php/jbi/article/view/12013
  The publisher page dates the article 2025-10-01; its PDF header says October
  2026. That discrepancy is preserved, not silently resolved. Only the stated
  classification is cited; no article figures, dataset, code, or model is reused.
- Selection is the intersection of available licensed labeled examples and
  corroborating hand/motion evidence. It is an initial subset, not full A-Z.

## What this does not establish

Dataset labels and literature support source comparison, not a universal or
community-approved standard. Region remains null; handedness policy remains
UNSPECIFIED. Do not infer that horizontal flips or hand substitutions preserve
meaning. No exact angles, distances, coaching corrections, or acceptance
thresholds were inferred from these pictures. The teaching instruction asks
learners to observe the referenced examples rather than asserting new pose rules.

The images illustrate variation; none is a universal perfect pose. Do not
equate matching this sample set with linguistic correctness for every signer.
Recognition acceptance requires Phase 4 calibration and live tests. Human
review, if obtained, must be recorded before using VALIDATOR_VERIFIED.

The references are instructional assets only. Learner camera input stays live,
local, and unrecorded. This is not an uploaded-video recognition feature.
